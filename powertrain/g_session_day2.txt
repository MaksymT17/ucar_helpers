1. Core Architectural Layout

    Body Style: Technical Notchback Sedan (3-box configuration).

    Structural Design: Fixed rear glass with a separate deck-lid (trunk). This maximizes torsional rigidity (>35,000 Nm/deg target) to handle high instantaneous motor torque.

    Dimensions: * Wheelbase: 2,800 mm (optimized for stability and interior volume).

        Ground Clearance: 170 mm (tailored for mixed-surface reliability and stewardship of the underbody).

    Mass Target: 1,800+ kg (Unladen/Kerb).

    Weight Distribution: 48/52 (Front/Rear) to optimize RWD traction and turn-in agility.

2. Powertrain & Hardware (The Muscle)
    Unit: Tesla Model 3 Rear Drive Unit (RDU), Refurbished.
    Peak Output: ∼210 kW (Motor-limited) / ∼450 Nm peak torque.
    Differential: Mechanical Open Differential (Integrated).
    Interface: High-speed CAN-bus (500 kbps).
    Integration: 12V-logic bypass via AEM VCU300 or OpenInverter logic board (Replacing proprietary Tesla firmware).

3. Digital Nervous System (The Brain)
    Primary VCU (AEM VCU300): Real-time torque management, startup/shutdown sequencing, and CAN-translation.

    Supervisory ECU (Renesas RH850/R-Car):
        Polling: Constant state-monitoring of BMS (SoC, Temp, Health).
        Logic: Execution of high-level "Arctic" simulation maps (Power limiting based on thermal/energy states).
        HMI: Drives the minimalist/driver-focused digital cockpit.

    API Command Set:
        Throttle: Torque Request (Nm) scaled by Drive Mode.
        Braking: Negative Torque Request (Nm) with zero-speed interlock.
        State: Direction (F/N/R) and Inverter Enable.

4. Dynamics & Control

    Braking: Hydraulic-only for Alpha Prototype (ABS-Ready provisioning).
        Software-Traction: Virtual slip control managed via the VCU to prevent rear lock-up during regeneration.

    Recuperation: * Logic: Dynamic negative torque mapping.
        Mode: "Hold" mode enabled (Active motor braking at 0 km/h for hill-start and stationary stability).

    Tuning: "Software-Defined Handling" via simulation-to-field porting (Tested on Arctic servers, validated on racing tracks).

5. Production & Stewardship
    Series Size: Founder’s Series of 15–20 units.
    Legal Pathway: Small Series / Individual Vehicle Approval (IVA).
    Ethics: Ethical sourcing of refurbished hardware to avoid slavery-linked supply chains.

    Business Model: "Systems Integrator"—acting as the connection point between Tier-1 hardware (Bosch/BorgWarner for future phases) and high-level software engineers.
