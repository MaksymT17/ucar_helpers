1. OVERVIEW: ASYMMETRIC AWD TOPOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Alpha EV prototype utilizes a strictly asymmetric dual-motor architecture. 
By intentionally pairing two distinct electromagnetic technologies (PMSM + ACIM), 
the vehicle maximizes highway cruising efficiency while enabling high-bias, 
forward-shifted kinetic recuperation and zero-drag freewheeling.

2. REAR AXLE: PRIMARY PROPULSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: Primary drive for all steady-state cruising and baseline acceleration.
Technology: Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor (PMSM).
Voltage Architecture: 400V DC Nominal.
Target Peak Output: ~210 kW / 3,600 Nm (at wheels).
Inverter Tech: Silicon Carbide (SiC) MOSFETs for >98% switching efficiency.

* Phase 1 (Alpha Prototype):
  Salvaged/Remanufactured Tesla Model 3 Rear Drive Unit (RDU).
  Controlled via 3rd-party VCU (e.g., AEM VCU300) or OpenInverter logic board.

* Phase 2 (Production Target):
  Bosch High-Performance PMSM eAxle.

3. FRONT AXLE: SECONDARY PROPULSION & RECUPERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: On-demand torque vectoring, primary kinetic recuperation, zero-drag idle.
Technology: AC Induction Motor (ACIM) / Asynchronous Machine (ASM).
Voltage Architecture: 400V DC Nominal.
Target Peak Output: ~60 kW / 1,200 Nm (at wheels).
Inverter Tech: Standard IGBT or SiC (depending on thermal/cost constraints).

* Phase 1 (Alpha Prototype):
  Salvaged/Remanufactured Tesla Model 3 Front Drive Unit (Induction).
  Selected specifically for its lack of permanent magnets (zero cogging).

* Phase 2 (Production Target):
  Bosch Secondary Drive eAxle (ASM or EESM).

4. CONTROL & INTEGRATION REQUIREMENTS (TIER-1 SUPPLIERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The inverter controller MUST expose a high-frequency, low-latency API (CAN-FD 
  or Automotive Ethernet).
- Must support >1000 Hz torque command polling.
- Internal resolver telemetry must bypass proprietary middleware to allow our 
  Supervisory ECU to execute sub-millisecond Virtual Slip Control (VSC).
